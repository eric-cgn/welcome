Gopher

Gopher is a protocol for finding your way through menus. A Gopher server offers a menu: a list of items, most of them documents to read, other menus, or searches. Menus are small and plain text, so they arrive quickly, even over a modem.

Try this

Type gopher://gopher.quux.org/ in the address bar and press Return.

Mill shows the server's main menu. Click Humor and Fun, and explore from there. To come back to a menu you've left, choose Back from the Go menu.

What it is

A Gopher menu looks much like a window in the Finder. Next to each item, Mill draws a small icon: a folder for another menu, a page for a document, and other icons for searches and files. Click a folder to open the menu inside it, and click a document to read it.

Unlike a Finder window, a menu can list items kept on other servers anywhere on the Internet. When you click one, Mill asks that server for it. You never need to know where an item lives; the menu knows for you.

A little history

Gopher was written at the University of Minnesota in 1991, and named after the university's mascot. Universities used it to put their libraries, phone books, and course listings on the Internet. Gopher is described in RFC 1436, published in 1993.

What's there

quux.org, at gopher://gopher.quux.org/, is kept by John Goerzen. It holds a large collection of documents, books, software, and humor, much of it about computers. Its menu Other Gopher Servers leads to many more.

Veronica-2 searches the menus of many Gopher servers at once. It is run by Cameron Kaiser at Floodgap Systems. Type gopher://gopher.floodgap.com/7/v2/vs, and Mill shows a box labeled Search for. Type a word, press Return, and Mill shows a menu of the items that match.

Good manners

Many Gopher servers are kept by one person or a small group, often at their own expense. If a server is slow to answer, it may be busy with other visitors. Try again a little later, rather than clicking over and over.

What Mill needs

Mill speaks Gopher with the Internet Services Plug-In, which Easy Install puts in the Mill Plug-Ins folder. A few kinds of Gopher item lead to things Mill can't open, and Mill leaves those out of the menu. To see pictures in the menu itself, each above its link, choose Preferences from the Edit menu and turn on Show images inline, under Gopher.
