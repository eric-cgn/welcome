Where to Go

Here are some places to start, grouped by protocol. Many are kept by people who share them because they enjoy it. Places on the Internet change from time to time; if one doesn't answer, try again later.

Addresses that don't begin with http need one of Mill's Plug-Ins, Internet Services. Easy Install, in Install Mill, puts it in place.

The World Wide Web

FrogFind
http://frogfind.com/
A search engine that also rewrites the pages it finds so a smaller computer can read them.
The World Wide Web project
http://info.cern.ch/hypertext/WWW/TheProject.html
The Web's early pages, kept by CERN, the laboratory in Geneva where the Web was invented.
68k.news
http://68k.news/
News headlines from around the world, set out plainly so a smaller computer can show them.
textfiles.com
http://www.textfiles.com/
A large library of text files collected from bulletin board systems, computers that people call by modem to trade messages and files.
Wiby
http://wiby.me/
A search engine for simple pages, many made by people writing about what interests them.

Gopher

Floodgap
gopher://gopher.floodgap.com/
A long-running Gopher server, with guides to Gopher and the Veronica-2 search engine.
SDF
gopher://sdf.org/
The SDF Public Access UNIX System, whose members publish Gopher menus of their own.
quux.org
gopher://gopher.quux.org/
Documents and rare files of historical interest, kept in Gopher so they aren't lost.

Files

Info-Mac at FUNET
ftp://ftp.funet.fi/pub/mac/info-mac/
A large collection of Macintosh software from the Info-Mac archive, kept by FUNET, the Finnish university network.
The GNU Project
ftp://ftp.gnu.org/
Free software, mostly for UNIX computers.

Finding People

Weather by finger
finger://graph.no/oslo
A weather forecast for Oslo, Norway, drawn in text. Put another city's name, such as london, in place of oslo.
Who looks after com
whois://whois.iana.org/com
Who keeps the list of names that end in com.

Reference

DICT
dict://dict.org/d:internet
A dictionary's definition of the word internet. Put another word in place of internet.
The time
time://time.nist.gov/
The time, from the National Institute of Standards and Technology in the United States. Mill can set your Macintosh's clock from it.

Newsgroups

Reading newsgroups takes a news server, and most ask you to sign up first. Newsgroups explains how.
