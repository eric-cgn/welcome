Welcome to the Internet

On behalf of OneInAMillion Software, welcome to the Internet.

If you are reading these words, you are already on it. Another computer sent this page to yours, and your computer could read it because both follow the same published rules. Those rules are what the Internet is made of, and this guide is about them.

I wrote this guide for people who use Mill, to help you make the most of it. A Compact Macintosh can do more on the Internet than most people expect. With Mill, it can browse the Web, explore Gopher menus, read newsgroups, fetch files, and more.

If you are reading on another kind of computer, you are welcome too. Nearly everything here applies to you as well, though you may need several programs to visit every place we describe. Mill users need only one.

Most of the pages that follow explain one part of the Internet and give you an address to try. Start with Rules of the Road, which explains how it all works. The rest can be read in any order.

Many of the places in this guide are kept by people who share them because they enjoy it. I hope you enjoy them as much.

Thank you for choosing Mill.

Sincerely,


Eric McIntyre
Founder, OneInAMillion Software
